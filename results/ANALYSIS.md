# Analysis

Generated by `python analysis/make_analysis.py` from `runs.csv`, `items.csv`, `calls.csv` and `samples.csv`. These are the tables of the technical report. Intervals are 95%: Wilson for shares, Newcombe for differences of shares, normal approximation for mean token counts ([analysis/stats.py](../analysis/stats.py)).

84 runs (6 of them idle measurements), 131,939 scored tasks, 210,054 model calls.

## Hypotheses (written before the runs, [docs/preregistration.md](../docs/preregistration.md))

| # | Hypothesis | Observed | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | At the operating point the card finishes at least 10 times the tasks per hour of one request at a time (short answer and agent question) | short answer x11.0, agent question x8.0 | **partly supported** |
| H2 | At least 80% of the agent's input tokens at the operating point come from the prefix cache, and throughput is at least twice that without it | 0.6% from cache at the operating point (29% at one agent); throughput with the cache x0.97 that without it | **not supported** |
| H3 | Accuracy at the operating point is within 5 percentage points of accuracy at one request, on all four tasks | largest difference 3.3 points | **supported** |
| H4 | Break-even with hardware only between 1,500 and 4,000 agent questions per working day, and above 10,000 short answers | 2,846 agent questions, 22,409 short answers | **supported** |
| H5 | One card's capacity at the operating point is at least 50 times the hardware-only break-even for agent questions | x37 over 24 hours, x12 over an 8-hour day | **not supported** |
